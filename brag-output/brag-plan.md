# Brag Plan: Roomtone

## What is this app?
Roomtone cleans up voice recordings in the browser, lets people shape the sound, and keeps their files on the device.

## The angle
Make the invisible work visible: a recording enters as a waveform, Roomtone brings the voice forward, then hands back a clean export without sending the file away. Keep the presentation polished and product-led, using the actual editor layout, names, controls, and privacy language.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Open on one clear line: “Make room for your voice.” A lime waveform resolves from scattered room noise into the Roomtone mark, then the actual editor appears.

## Key moments (the middle)
- A recording is dropped into the editor and appears as a real waveform.
- Original switches to Enhanced as the AI noise reduction control moves.
- The Natural, Bright, and Warm starting points and “Quiet the pauses” control show how the sound can be shaped.
- WAV and MP3 exports appear beside the “Files stay on this device” message.

## Outro / punchline
Roomtone wordmark with the product’s privacy promise: “Your files stay on this device.”

## User flow worth showing
Choose a recording → reduce noise and shape its voice → compare Original with Enhanced → download WAV or MP3. Keep the flow at the center of the video.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: a quiet, polished product film using Roomtone’s own UI and green identity
- Interpretation: clean framing, confident holds, and light motion; the interface and local-processing promise carry the story without exaggerated claims.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20.75 seconds

## Audio direction
- Role: steady, clean bed with a few restrained interface accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
- Music treatment: around 0.30 volume, quick soft fade-in, gentle fade under the final wordmark; let the interface clicks remain clear.
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`, estimated 109.96 BPM. Major visual moments may land near 8.74s and 17.47s; beats fall roughly every 0.55s. Use beat timing only when it preserves readable holds.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; let the existing lime indicator or card presence breathe with the music. No waveform/equalizer graphics beyond the actual product waveform.
- SFX posture: sparse and soft; one file-selection click, one Enhanced-state confirmation, and a quiet logo landing if the motion supports it.
- Audio-coupled moments: the editor appears with a soft reveal; Original-to-Enhanced switches on a clean accent; the export buttons and final wordmark settle on strong beats.
- Restraint rule: no loud whooshes, bright repeated ticks, voiceover, or heavy pulsing.
