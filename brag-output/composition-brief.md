# Roomtone Product Film — Composition Brief

## Deliverable

- 20.75 seconds, landscape 1920 × 1080
- Product-led, polished, quietly upbeat
- Music bed: bundled happy-beats business moves, volume 12; subdued under interface moments
- No voiceover
- Keep all text legible and use only fictional example media (`voice-note.wav`)

## Story

1. **Make room** — A fine lime signal resolves into the Roomtone mark. Headline: “Make room for your voice.”
2. **Bring in a take** — A fictional `voice-note.wav` enters the editor. Show “Drop a recording to get started” and “Runs in your browser.”
3. **Clear the voice** — Show the editor waveform, Original/Enhanced comparison, and AI noise reduction at the interface's 48% setting. Headline: “Reduce background noise.”
4. **Shape the sound** — Show Natural, Bright, and Warm; select Warm, and reveal Quiet the pauses, Voice presence, and Volume balance.
5. **Take it with you** — Close on the Roomtone mark and “Your files stay on this device,” with WAV and MP3 export formats settling into view.

## Audio
