# Roomtone Brag Film Design

## Style Prompt

A polished, product-led film for Roomtone. Keep the interface recognizable and calm: warm workspace surfaces, a deep forest sidebar, Roomtone's lime signal, and confident breathing room. Show the real workflow through a believable editor mockup, with restrained movement that keeps controls and privacy copy readable.

## Colors

- Workspace background: `#f4f6f2`
- Forest green: `#17352d`
- Secondary green: `#244c3d`
- Main text: `#20332d`
- Roomtone lime: `#c8ea78`
- Card surface: `#ffffff`
- Divider: `#e2e7e1`

## Typography

- Headlines and brand: Space Grotesk
- Interface and supporting copy: DM Sans

## Motion

- Calm, deliberate transitions with short ease-out entrances and readable holds.
- Let a fine signal resolve into the brand, then move attention through the product flow.
- Use small scale, opacity, and position changes; keep the interface steady while one feature is highlighted at a time.
- Keep ambient movement slow and subtle.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not invent product claims or expose real user recordings or names.
- Do not use a generic audio visualizer in place of Roomtone's editor waveform.
- Do not add neon gradients, heavy glows, loud whooshes, or rapid UI motion.
- Do not reduce contrast or obscure controls with decorative overlays.
- Do not change the product's stated local-processing promise.
