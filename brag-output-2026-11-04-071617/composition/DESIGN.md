# Roomtone Brag Film Design

## Style Prompt

A cinematic but precise product film for Roomtone. Frame the real interface like a thoughtfully lit object: warm canvas, deep forest rails, lime signal details, confident editorial type, and visible workspace depth. The motion should feel memorable through exact match moves, layered panels, and deliberate holds while the controls remain recognizable.

## Colors

- Workspace background: `#f4f6f2`
- Forest green: `#17352d`
- Secondary green: `#244c3d`
- Main text: `#20332d`
- Roomtone lime: `#c8ea78`
- Card surface: `#ffffff`
- Divider: `#e2e7e1`

## Typography

- Headlines and brand: Space Grotesk
- Interface and supporting copy: DM Sans
- Cue timecodes: ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Consolas, monospace

## Motion

- Use a consistent editorial push between related screens and a slower, warm resolve into the final card.
- Treat the Roomtone interface as the subject: crisp close crops, a restrained perspective shift, and clean parallax between workspace, card, and controls.
- Use small lime accents to guide the eye from recording to enhanced audio to timed captions.
- Preserve settled reading time for headings, quality options, and the privacy line.
- Keep audio-reactive motion to a subtle response on the existing signal mark.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not invent product claims or expose real user recordings or names.
- Do not use a generic audio visualizer in place of Roomtone's editor waveform.
- Do not use neon gradients, heavy glows, strobing, aggressive whooshes, or rapid UI motion.
- Do not obscure controls with decoration or shrink interface text below legible video sizes.
- Do not change the product's local-processing promise.
